Scenario A · Markup on the WS fee

  • Every product's fee rises by a flat {{MARKUP}}. Commissions come out of the new fee.
  • Dealer sees a small, uniform increase. WS keeps the difference after payouts.
  • Overall commission falls from {{WAS_PCT}} of the original fee to {{A_PCT}} of the new fee.
    • {{NEG_NOTE}}

Scenario B · Commission added to product cost

  • WS fee stays exactly at the Vantage amount. The three payouts are added to the product's cost.
  • WS nets the full Vantage fee on every contract; nothing is carved out.
  • Dealer increase varies by product: $3 on the $8 and $10 items, $9 on $15, $15 on $25 and $30, $21 on $35 and on the Preferred Care Advantage 2 - 7 Year exception.

Decision requested

  • Approve the per-tier payout amounts to Paul, Ken, and Ryan.
  • Choose Scenario A or Scenario B for funding the payouts.
