# DriveOne Direct ads, captions

One caption per concept. Both directions of a concept share a caption, because
the direction test is a creative test and a different caption would confound it.

**Meta field targets:** primary text reads under 125 characters before the
"see more" fold, headline under 40, description under 30. The boilerplate sits
below the fold, which is fine on every placement except Reels.

## Boilerplate

Paste as the last block of every caption, above hashtags:

> DriveOne Direct is a brand of Insureify AI, Inc. Vehicle service contracts are not insurance, a warranty, or a guarantee. Administrator and obligor: Ascent Administration Services, LLC, Tempe, AZ; administrator and obligor vary by state. Obligations are insured under a contractual liability insurance policy issued by Old Republic Insurance Company. Coverage and eligibility subject to contract terms and exclusions. Available in all states except California.

Hashtags (rotate 5 to 7, never in the image):
`#vehicleservicecontract #carrepair #usedcars #carmaintenance #autorepair #carowner #roadtrip`

## Do not write

**Meta Personal Attributes.** This is the live risk on this account, not a
theoretical one. C1B was disapproved on 19 Aug 2026 for copy that narrated the
viewer's own situation, while C12 passed using the identical photograph with a
general framing. State the category truth, never assert or imply knowledge of
the reader's life.

Avoid: "your warranty just expired", "you just paid for", "since your car has",
"you're driving without coverage", "your repair bill", and any second-person
sentence that claims to know the reader's circumstances, vehicle, finances or
credit.

Use instead: "the factory warranty ends", "a replacement transmission runs",
"cooling system parts fail", "coverage after the warranty".

**Meta Credit special ad category.** No payment plan, 0%, no-credit-check, APR,
financing or loan language anywhere. Ryan's call: the targeting restrictions are
not worth the claim on prospecting.

**Product truth.** Never call DriveOne's product a warranty. It is a vehicle
service contract, or coverage. "Warranty" refers only to the factory warranty
the buyer no longer has. No competitor names, no testimonials, no star ratings,
no urgency or scarcity, no price or savings claims beyond contract terms.

**Claims with no contract behind them.** Diminished Value, Openbay, the
Protection Plus bundle, "ASE-certified", and any coverage term limit. See
`docs/claims-audit.md` for why each is out.

**Never use em dashes or en dashes.** Commas, colons, parentheses or separate
sentences.
