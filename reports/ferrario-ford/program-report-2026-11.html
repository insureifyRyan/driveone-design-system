Who is eligible, and why the lists shrink

A customer receives emails only when all of the following hold. The same rules apply to every audience.

  • The vehicle is model year 2014 or newer. This is the largest filter on the two new lists: the recall visitors have a median model year of 2012, and the drive-by list the same, so most of those vehicles are simply too old to cover.
  • There is no vehicle service contract already on file.
  • We have a working email address, and the person has not opted out, bounced, or marked a previous email as spam.
  • The vehicle is not a heavy-duty truck. Nothing above the F-150 class is rated in this program (F-250 and up, RAM 2500 and up, GM 2500 and 3500), so those customers never receive an email.
  • Nobody receives two series. A person who appears in more than one list is emailed once, in the first group they qualified for.

What Ferrario Ford should expect

  • Calls to the service line. Every email prints (607) 734-1681. A customer may ring to ask whether the email is genuine. It is, and the price in it is theirs.
  • Replies. Replies go to DriveOne, who answer coverage questions and pass anything about a specific vehicle to the store.
  • Opt-outs. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Opt-outs, bounces and spam complaints remove the person from the program automatically, across all three groups.
  • Sales. A purchase is posted to the store the moment it completes. The customer's contract names Ferrario Ford as the selling dealer and carries the $0 in-store deductible.
  • This report. Refreshed on the first of each month with emails sent, quote clicks, contracts sold and opt-outs for each group.
